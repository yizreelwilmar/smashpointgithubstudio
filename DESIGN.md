# SMASHPOINT Design System & Direction

## 1. Identity & Mood
- **Brand Name**: SMASHPOINT
- **Domain**: Badminton, Padel, Tennis, & Pickleball Community Management, Court Rotation & Live Leaderboards.
- **Mood**: High-precision sports engineering, editorial athletic craftsmanship, tactile court simulation.
- **Personality**: Energetic yet disciplined, fair, modern, enterprise-reliable.

## 2. Color Palette & Purpose
- **Court Deep Slate** (`#090E17` / `#0F172A`): Deep stadium foundation, absorbs glare, perfect for court visualization.
- **Court Surface Neutral** (`#1E293B` dark / `#FFFFFF` light): Crisp contrast, tactical board readability.
- **Court Accent Volt / Grass** (`#10B981` & `#10E599`): Active play indicator, winning team badge, live match ticker.
- **Bench Alert Amber** (`#F59E0B`): Waiting queue, bye tracker, player rest alerts.
- **Subtle Outline** (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `#E2E8F0`): Clean separation without muddy drop shadows.

## 3. Typography
- **Headlines & Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans (900/800/700 weight, tracking-tight) for athletic punch and authority.
- **Numbers & Scores**: Monospace / Tabular figures with bold weight for split-second scoreboard readability.
- **Body & Data**: Inter (400/500/600) for high legibility at 12-14px in complex player roster tables.

## 4. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY**: 3 (Dynamic court layouts, live status badges, interactive scoreboard controls)
- **RHYTHM**: 3 (Consistent 8px spatial grid, high-contrast typography hierarchy, alternating court cards)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Deliberate micro-interactions: hover score increments, active button depression, smooth tab switches)
